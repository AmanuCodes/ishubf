    About Us

    Empowering Innovation Through Digital Products & Services

    About Our Company

    Sparkling Solutions is a forward-thinking, private technology company established in 2026. Founded and directed by Mr. Amanuel, our firm specializes in crafting high-efficiency software applications, educational digital infrastructure, healthcare systems, and comprehensive technical advisory services.

    We aim to solve modern digital problems through streamlined, highly accessible software tools. Operating across multiple sectors, Sparkling Solutions continues to build systems that prioritize performance, clarity, and ease of deployment.

    Company History & Growth

    Starting in early 2026 with a dedicated focus on educational platforms, our initial release—Sparkling Study—provided digital organizing systems for students and academic institutions. Recognizing the growing demand for customized software solutions, Sparkling Solutions expanded rapidly into health platforms and enterprise business products.

    Key Timeline Highlights

    • Q1 2026: Official launch of Sparkling Solutions by Founder Mr. Amanuel.
    • Q2 2026: Deployment of our flagship education tools: Sparkling Study, Sparkling Class, and Sparkling Libra.
    • Q3 2026: Expansion into Health Tech with Sparkling Med & TeleMedicine integration.
    • Present & Future: Rollout of financial tools (Sparkling Coin, Sparkling Econ) and dedicated advisory services.

    Our Core Values

    Every line of code and service package we deliver is guided by five foundational principles:

    1. Simplicity: Creating functional, clean interfaces without unnecessary bloat.
    2. Security: Ensuring user data and operational privacy are protected at every level.
    3. Accessibility: Designing products that function across mobile and lightweight environments seamlessly.
    4. Efficiency: High-speed delivery with low system overhead.
    5. Customer-Centric Growth: Continually updating applications based on direct user feedback.
