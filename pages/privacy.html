    Privacy Policy

    At Sparkling Solutions, we keep data collection minimal, secure, and straightforward. This policy outlines how your information is handled when using our services.

    1. Information We Collect

    When you contact us or set up an account, we collect standard information such as your name, email address, phone number, user role (e.g., student or organization), and any message details you submit.

    2. How We Use Your Data

    Your details are strictly used to reply to inquiries, schedule mentoring or telemedicine sessions, fulfill digital product subscriptions, and send important service updates. We do not sell or trade your data to third-party marketers.

    3. Data Protection

    We use localized data storage options and standard encryption protocols across our web applications to safeguard your account details and consultation records from unauthorized access.

    4. Your Rights

    You can request to update your personal details or delete your user account at any time by sending a message through our primary contact form.

    Have questions about your data? Contact our privacy team.
